🔬 Academic Prototype Notice
Module Outside Research Scope

This web application is an independent academic research simulation developed for an Information Systems Management (ISM) Master's thesis at VŠE.

The research focuses on the AI Student Assistant (VŠE Buddy Bot) and authenticated study administration (coursework drop-boxes, ECTS credit tracking, thesis filing). Public institutional databases (such as staff directories, card validity systems, and external forms) are intentionally out of scope.

💡 How to continue evaluating the prototype:
  • Student Portal Experience: Click Log in to Personal administration (any name, password: ism_mastersthesis).
  • Public Inquiries: Open the VŠE Buddy Bot in the bottom-right corner to ask about admissions, visas, dorms, and campus policies without an account!

🧪 Academic Master's Thesis Prototype • Non-Official Simulation

This platform is an independent academic research and development simulation created by an Information Systems Management (ISM) Master's student at the Faculty of Informatics and Statistics (FIS), Prague University of Economics and Business (VŠE).

Research Purpose: Developed exclusively for thesis research, architectural proof-of-concept, and usability evaluation of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) copilots for student support in higher education.
Non-Affiliation Notice: This simulation is not operated, hosted, or endorsed as an official service by VŠE administration. It contains mock and public demonstration data only.
