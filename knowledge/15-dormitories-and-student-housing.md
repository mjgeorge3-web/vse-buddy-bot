# VŠE dormitories and student housing (SÚZ VŠE)

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.

## Overview and Management
VŠE student dormitories are operated and managed by the Facilities Management Department (Správa účelových zařízení, SÚZ VŠE, formerly SKM). The official accommodation web portal is https://accommodation.vse.cz/ and the booking system is ISKaM at https://iskam-web.vse.cz/.

## Dormitory Locations and Campuses
VŠE offers student accommodation across multiple dormitories grouped primarily into two student campuses:
- **Jarov Campus (Prague 3 - Žižkov / Jarov):**
  - Palachova dormitory, Eislerova dormitory, Thalerova dormitory, University Hotel, and Jarov II.
  - Located approximately 10–15 minutes from the main VŠE campus in Žižkov by direct tram (lines 9, 26, 31 to tram stop Chmelnice or Strážní).
  - Very popular among degree students and international exchange students.
- **Jižní Město Campus (Prague 4 - Chodov / Kunratice):**
  - Blanice dormitory and Vltava dormitory.
  - Located directly at the Jižní Město satellite campus near metro line C (stations Chodov / Opatov) and bus stop Volha.
  - Houses both Czech and international students with dining halls and extensive outdoor sports areas nearby.
- **Other locations:** Rooseveltova dormitory (Prague 7 - Holešovice).

## How to Apply for Dormitory Accommodation via ISKaM
> **UNVERIFIED (Seasonal Dates & Prices):** Exact monthly dormitory fees, deposit amounts, and semester booking deadlines change each academic year and are not verified in real time. Always confirm current prices and deadlines directly on https://accommodation.vse.cz/ or with SÚZ VŠE (`ubytovani@vse.cz`).

1. **Access ISKaM Portal:** Log in to the ISKaM accommodation portal at https://iskam-web.vse.cz/ using your VŠE InSIS username and password.
2. **Submit Housing Request:** Complete and submit the online accommodation application during the designated registration window for international or incoming students.
3. **Select Room and Dormitory:** Choose from available room types (typically 2-bed rooms in apartment units or shared rooms).
4. **Pay Reservation Deposit (Kauce):** Pay the required refundable accommodation deposit via credit card or international bank transfer before the stated deadline. The room booking is only finalized once the deposit is confirmed in ISKaM.
5. **Move-in and Accommodation Contract:** Upon arrival, visit the dormitory reception or accommodation manager, present your passport/ID, sign the official Accommodation Contract (*Ubytovací smlouva*), and receive your room keys.

## Room Amenities and Facilities
- All rooms are furnished with beds, study desks, chairs, wardrobes, and bed linen (regular linen laundry exchange is included in the fee).
- **Wi-Fi and Internet:** High-speed campus Wi-Fi (**eduroam**) is available across all dormitories for VŠE students.
- **Kitchen and Sanitary Facilities:** Depending on the dormitory type, bathrooms and kitchenettes are either located inside private apartment units (cell blocks) or shared on the floor.
- **Common Amenities:** Self-service laundry rooms equipped with washing machines and dryers (booked through ISKaM), quiet study rooms, and bike storage.

## Automatic Foreign Police Registration for Dormitory Residents
International students residing in a VŠE university dormitory benefit from automatic reporting:
- SÚZ VŠE registers your arrival and stay with the Czech Foreign Police (*Cizinecká policie*) electronically on your behalf.
- Non-EU and EU students living in dorms do **not** need to visit the Foreign Police in person at Olšanská to report their arrival, provided they check in with valid identification.
- An official proof of accommodation (*Doklad o zajištění ubytování*) required for visa or residence permit applications can be requested directly from SÚZ VŠE.
