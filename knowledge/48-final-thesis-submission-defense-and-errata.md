# Final thesis submission, InSIS errata, defense and reviews at VŠE

> General information only. Always check specific faculty and department deadlines in InSIS and consult your thesis supervisor (*vedoucí práce*) or faculty Study Department.

## 1. Overview of Final Theses at VŠE
At VŠE (including the Faculty of Informatics and Statistics - FIS, and degree programmes like Information Systems Management - ISM), completing a Bachelor's or Master's programme requires writing, submitting, and defending a final thesis:
- **Bachelor’s Thesis (Bakalářská práce):** Recommended length approx. 10,000 words (around 35–50 standard pages).
- **Master’s / Diploma Thesis (Diplomová práce):** Recommended length approx. 20,000 words (around 60–80 standard pages).
- **Language:** Written in English for English-taught programmes (such as ISM, CEMS, MIMG, IBB, EDS) or Czech/Slovak for Czech programmes.
- **Templates:** Official VŠE templates (Word and LaTeX) with required ordering, APA citation styles, and title pages are available on the faculty website and InSIS.

---

## 2. Thesis Topic Assignment (Zadání závěrečné práce)
1. **Topic Selection:** Choose an advertised topic in InSIS under **Student's Portal** → **Thesis Topics (Témata závěrečných prací)**, or agree on an individual research topic with a prospective supervisor (*vedoucí práce*).
2. **Assignment Protocol:** The supervisor enters the formal assignment into InSIS:
   - Official title in English and Czech.
   - Objectives and methodology (*Zásady pro vypracování*).
   - Preliminary table of contents and primary literature/sources.
3. **Approval:** The assignment is electronically approved in InSIS by the student, supervisor, head of department (*vedoucí katedry*), and dean/vice-dean. This must be completed well before final submission (typically 3–6 months in advance).

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Electronic Thesis Submission in InSIS
Final theses at VŠE are submitted **electronically via InSIS**. Printed hard copies are generally no longer required for defense, unless specifically requested by your department.

1. **Open InSIS:** Log in at https://insis.vse.cz/ with your VŠE credentials.
2. **Navigate to Submission Portal:** Go to **Student's Portal** (*Portál studenta*) → **Final Thesis** (*Závěrečná práce*).
3. **Fill Required Metadata:**
   - English and Czech thesis title (must match the approved assignment).
   - English and Czech abstract (*Abstrakt*).
   - English and Czech keywords (*Klíčová slova*).
4. **Upload Thesis File:**
   - Upload the full text of the thesis strictly in **PDF format** (searchable text, PDF/A standard recommended, no password protection).
   - The file must contain all formal sections: Title page, Declaration of Authorship / Originality, Table of Contents, Chapters, Reference List, and Appendices.
5. **Upload Supplementary Attachments:**
   - If your thesis involves practical software prototypes, code, datasets, or survey materials, package them into a `.zip` archive and upload under supplementary files.
6. **Confirm Final Submission:**
   - Click the final submission button (**Potvrdit odevzdání** / Confirm Submission).
   - Verify the system confirmation message: *"Elektronická verze závěrečné práce byla odevzdána a bylo potvrzeno její korektní vložení. Proces vkládání je tímto ukončen. Práci již nelze modifikovat."*
   - ⚠️ **Important Warning:** Once final submission is confirmed, the system locks your file. You cannot replace or edit the uploaded PDF directly.

## 4. How to Submit Errata in InSIS (Errata k závěrečné práci)
If you discover factual errors, typos, calculation mistakes, or formatting inaccuracies **after** your thesis has already been locked and submitted in InSIS, you can submit an official **Errata** (*Errata k práci*):

### What is Errata?
Under the Higher Education Act (§ 47b) and VŠE Study Regulations, submitted theses become public academic records. An **Errata** (Erratum) is an official supplementary document listing specific corrections to errors identified in the submitted thesis without altering the locked original file.

### How to Submit Errata in InSIS (Step-by-Step):
1. **Log in to InSIS:** Go to https://insis.vse.cz/.
2. **Access Final Thesis Application:** Navigate to **Student's Portal** (*Portál studenta*) → **Final Thesis** (*Závěrečná práce*).
3. **Find Errata Option:** In the student editing section (*Editační aplikace studenta*), locate the button **"Insert Errata"** (**Vložit errata**).
4. **Upload Errata File:** Upload your structured corrections document strictly in **PDF format**.
5. **Supervisor Consultation:** Always inform and consult your thesis supervisor (*vedoucí práce*) before uploading. The supervisor reviews the corrections and acknowledges them in InSIS.
6. **Publication:** Once confirmed, the Errata file is permanently attached to your final thesis record in InSIS and Theses.cz, making the corrections visible to the examination committee, opponent, and public readers.

### Recommended Errata Document Format:
Prepare a clear 1–2 page PDF document structured as follows:
- **Header:**
  - University: Prague University of Economics and Business (VŠE)
  - Faculty and Programme (e.g. FIS – Information Systems Management)
  - Thesis Title: [Full Title of Your Thesis]
  - Author: [Your Full Name and Student ID]
  - Supervisor: [Supervisor's Name and Academic Titles]
  - Date: [Date of Errata Preparation]
- **Correction Table:**
  | Page / Section | Original (Erroneous) Text | Corrected (Accurate) Text | Reason / Note |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | Page 24, Table 2 | "Values in EUR thousands" | "Values in CZK thousands" | Unit label correction |
  | Page 48, Table 4 | "Value = 12.4%" | "Value = 18.2%" | Calculation adjustment |
- **Minor Errors at Defense:** For very minor typos or grammar glitches, you do not always need a formal Errata upload; you can prepare a brief slide in your defense presentation acknowledging the corrections in response to the opponent's review.

## 5. Automated Plagiarism Check (Theses.cz / VŠE Validator)
- After submission in InSIS, your thesis is automatically sent to the Czech national repository **Theses.cz** for similarity analysis against academic papers, published theses, and web pages.
- The similarity protocol is generated in InSIS within a few hours to days.
- Both your supervisor and opponent evaluate the similarity percentage and verify that all external sources and quotes are properly referenced according to academic ethics and VŠE AI guidelines.

---

## 6. Supervisor and Opponent Reviews (Posudky)
- **Two Written Reviews:** Your thesis is evaluated independently by your supervisor (*Posudek vedoucího*) and an assigned opponent (*Posudek oponenta*).
- **Evaluation Criteria:** Fulfillment of thesis objectives, theoretical framework, methodology, analytical rigor, formal formatting, and original contribution.
- **Grade Proposal:** Both reviews propose a grade: *Výborně* (1 - Excellent), *Velmi dobře* (2 - Very Good), *Dobře* (3 - Good), or *Nevyhověl* (4 - Failed).
- **Questions for Defense:** The reviews include specific questions (*otázky k obhajobě*) which you must address during your defense presentation.
- **Deadline for Reviews:** Reviews must be published in InSIS at least **3 working days** before the defense date, giving you time to prepare your slides and answers.

---

## 7. Thesis Defense Presentation (Obhajoba)
1. **Prerequisites:** All required coursework and ECTS credits completed (180 ECTS for Bachelor, 120 ECTS for Master), thesis submitted on time.
2. **Registration:** Register for the defense date under **Student's Portal** → **State Final Exams (SZZ / Státnice)**.
3. **Presentation:** Prepare a 5–10 minute presentation (PowerPoint or PDF slides):
   - Background, motivation, and practical relevance.
   - Research questions / objectives.
   - Methodology and dataset / prototype implementation.
   - Key findings, evaluation metrics, and thesis contribution.
4. **Defense Procedure:**
   - Introduction of candidate and thesis title by the committee chair.
   - Student delivers the presentation.
   - Reading of the supervisor's and opponent's reviews.
   - Student presents direct, prepared answers to the review questions.
   - Open discussion and questions from committee members.
   - Closed committee deliberation and final grade announcement.
