# Czech culture, history and student traditions

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
Czech Republic sits in Central Europe with Germanic, Slavic and Habsburg influences. The Orientation Week "Cultural History of Czech Lands" lecture is the recommended primer.

Traditions to know: Christmas markets (Advent), Easter whip tradition (pomlázka), beer culture (Czech pubs are family-friendly at lunch), name days, anniversaries of 1989 Velvet Revolution on 17 November.

Recommended Czech films (VŠE Ambassadors Instagram, 2026-10): Anthropoid, Waves (Vlny), Kolya (Kolja).
