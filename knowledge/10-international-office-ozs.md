# International Office (OZS) – coordination of international mobility

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
The Office for International Relations (OZS) at VŠE manages Erasmus+, bilateral exchanges, CESP free movers, photo contests and study-abroad communication channels.

News for outgoing/incoming students and staff is published at https://ozs.vse.cz/english/news-for-students/ and .../news-for-teaching-staff/.

For personal guidance, contact the International Office through the official VŠE directory: https://insis.vse.cz/lide/?lang=en (switchboard +420 224 095 111).
