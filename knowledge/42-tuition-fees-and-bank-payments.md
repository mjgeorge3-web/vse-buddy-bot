## How to pair payments: InSIS Variable Symbol (VS)
- Every student has a unique **Variable Symbol (VS)** generated automatically in the InSIS portal under **Student Portal → Study Financing (Financování studia)**.
- **Mandatory:** You must always include your exact Variable Symbol in the payment details. Without the Variable Symbol, the university accounting system cannot pair the payment with your student account.
- **Payment message / note for receiver:** Enter your full name and surname together with your Variable Symbol in the message field.
- **Bank transfer charges:** Always select the **OUR** option (sender bears all bank transfer charges). If intermediary or receiving bank charges are deducted from the tuition amount, the payment is treated as incomplete.
- **Card and QR payment:** Students can also pay tuition or application fees directly by card through the InSIS payment gateway, or generate a Czech QR code for instant domestic transfer.

## Tuition fee deadlines and policies
- Tuition for English-taught degree programmes typically ranges from 5,000 EUR per academic year (approx. 2,500 EUR per semester), payable before the beginning of each semester.
- Exact due dates are specified in your admission letter and in InSIS under Study Financing.
- Failure to pay tuition on time may lead to suspension of course registration or termination of study.
