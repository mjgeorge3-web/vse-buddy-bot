# InSIS course registration, rounds and credit voucher system at VŠE

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.

## VŠE credit voucher system
- VŠE uses a virtual credit voucher system to measure study progress and allow flexibility.
- Each incoming student receives a voucher pool:
  - **Bachelor's students:** 216 vouchers (180 for standard degree requirements + 36 reserve vouchers = 20% safety margin).
  - **Master's students:** 144 vouchers (120 for standard degree requirements + 24 reserve vouchers = 20% safety margin).
- **How vouchers work:** 1 ECTS credit = 1 voucher. When enrolling in a course, the corresponding vouchers are deducted. Passing the course converts those vouchers into earned ECTS credits. Failing an enrolled course consumes the vouchers permanently without earning credits.
- If a student runs out of vouchers and has fewer vouchers remaining than ECTS needed to graduate, studies are terminated.

## Course registration and enrollment rounds
Registration in InSIS is split into clearly separated phases:
1. **Pre-registration (Orders):** Students express interest by ordering compulsory and elective courses. This phase is **not first-come, first-served**; submitting on day 1 or the final day gives equal priority.
2. **Automated processing:** InSIS allocates seats based on priority rules (curriculum requirements, earned credits, study plan group).
3. **Enrollment Round 1:** Management and confirmation of compulsory courses.
4. **Enrollment Round 2:** Enrollment of compulsory and pre-registered optional/elective courses.
5. **Enrollment Round 3 (Open changes):** Students can enroll in any open course across faculties that has remaining free capacity, even if not pre-registered.
6. **Add/Drop period:** Changes can still be made during the first week of teaching in the semester.

## Minimum semester credits and exam retakes
- **First-semester threshold:** Students must earn at least **20 ECTS credits** in their first semester to remain eligible for subsequent semester enrollment. Standard full-time study load is 30 ECTS per semester (60 ECTS per academic year).
- **Exam retakes:** If a student fails an exam on the regular attempt, they are entitled to up to **two retake attempts** (opravný termín) within the examination period of the same semester.
