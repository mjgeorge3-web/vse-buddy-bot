# VŠE Campus Offices, Study Coordinators, Libraries & Main Halls

> Verified VŠE administrative offices, libraries, dining facilities, and ceremonial halls across the Žižkov, Jižní Město, and Jarov/SÚZ campuses. Always confirm seasonal opening hours on the linked official VŠE page.

## Central Library Žižkov (Knihovna VŠE / CIKS) (SB Central Library (CIKS))
- **Building & Floor:** SB — Mezzanine
- **How to Find It:** Enter Stará budova (SB) from náměstí Winstona Churchilla 4 (trams 5, 9, 15, 26 to Viktoria Žižkov or bus 135 to Náměstí Winstona Churchilla). Follow campus signage up to the mezzanine level of the Old Building (SB) for the Central Library entrance and Bibliobox book return.
- **Services & Contacts:** Reading rooms, book checkout, Bibliobox return, catalogue terminals, and SafeQ printing/copying. Contact: knihovna@vse.cz, +420 224 095 581.
- **Hours:** Mon 09:00-19:00, Tue-Thu 08:00-19:00, Fri 08:00-18:00 (confirm current seasonal hours on https://library.vse.cz/)
- **Official Page:** https://library.vse.cz/

## IT Support Helpdesk — Centrum informatiky (CI) (Rooms SB 22/23) (SB 22 / SB 23)
- **Building & Floor:** SB — Ground / Mezzanine
- **How to Find It:** Enter Stará budova (SB) from náměstí Winstona Churchilla and follow interior signage to rooms SB 22/23 (Centrum informatiky student IT helpdesk).
- **Services & Contacts:** InSIS password recovery, eduroam Wi-Fi help, Microsoft 365, and basic IT support. Contact: helpdesk@vse.cz, +420 224 095 890 (https://ci.vse.cz/).
- **Hours:** Confirm current walk-in hours on https://ci.vse.cz/
- **Official Page:** https://ci.vse.cz/english/services/for-employees/help-desk/

- **Building & Floor:** SB — Ground / 0
- **How to Find It:** Located in Stará budova (SB) on the Žižkov campus. Follow entrance signage for the Student ID Card Centre and confirm counter hours and photo upload instructions at https://ci.vse.cz/english/services/other/id-cards/.

## Likešova Aula (Stará budova Ceremonial Lecture Hall) (Likešova aula)
- **Building & Floor:** SB — Old Building
- **How to Find It:** Located inside Stará budova (SB) on the main Žižkov campus. Follow interior directional signs inside SB to Likešova aula.
- **Services & Contacts:** Large lecture hall used for faculty lectures, academic ceremonies, and examinations.
- **Hours:** Open during scheduled lectures and university events
- **Official Page:** https://www.vse.cz/english/students/campus-services/campus-and-classrooms/

## Vencovského Aula (Main University Auditorium) (Vencovského aula)
- **Building & Floor:** NB — Ground / 0
- **How to Find It:** Enter Nová budova (NB) from the central Žižkov campus courtyard or via the connecting passage from SB/RB. Vencovského aula is the main auditorium off the NB ground-floor atrium.
- **Services & Contacts:** Main ceremonial auditorium used for matriculation, graduation, keynote lectures, and large exams; barrier-free access available.
- **Hours:** Open for scheduled lectures, exams, and university ceremonies
- **Official Page:** https://www.vse.cz/english/students/campus-services/campus-and-classrooms/

## VŠE Student Canteen & Dining (Menza Žižkov — AV GASTRO) (Žižkov Menza (AV GASTRO / SÚZ))
- **Building & Floor:** IB / Žižkov — Ground / Campus Dining
- **How to Find It:** Located on the main Žižkov campus (Italská budova / central campus dining complex). Operated by AV GASTRO, s.r.o. under SÚZ VŠE; menus and meal ordering are available via WebKredit / MobilKredit2 and https://www.vse.cz/english/students/campus-services/catering-and-menus/.
- **Services & Contacts:** Subsidized student hot meals and snacks payable with student ID/ISIC card or payment card; microwave zones and cafés also available on campus.
- **Hours:** Confirm daily lunch hours and menus on https://www.vse.cz/english/students/campus-services/catering-and-menus/
- **Official Page:** https://www.vse.cz/english/students/campus-services/catering-and-menus/

## Academic Psychological Counselling (AC VŠE) (AC VŠE (ac@vse.cz))
- **Building & Floor:** Žižkov Campus — By Appointment
- **How to Find It:** Sessions are held in person on the Žižkov campus or online via MS Teams by prior email reservation at ac@vse.cz. The exact consultation room is provided upon booking confirmation.
- **Services & Contacts:** Free, strictly confidential psychological counselling in English and Czech (https://ac.vse.cz/english/psychological-counselling/).
- **Hours:** By prior email appointment via ac@vse.cz
- **Official Page:** https://ac.vse.cz/english/psychological-counselling/
