# Visa and residence permit for study in the Czech Republic – general information

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
## General information only – verify with the official authorities
Non-EU/EEA students typically need either:
- **Long-stay visa for study (90+ days)**, applied at a Czech embassy/consulate before arrival.
- **Long-stay residence permit for study**, applied at the Czech embassy/consulate (renewable in Czechia).

The decision depends on the length and type of study. As of 2026-10-25 the live URL https://www.mvcr.cz/mvcren/article/long-stay-visa.aspx could not be verified live in this run – marked as a gap below.

EU/EEA/Swiss students do not need a visa but must register with the Foreign Police if staying >30 days and register for health insurance.

Action: always consult the **VŠE International Office** and the **Czech Ministry of the Interior (mvcr.cz)** for your personal case. Do not rely on third-party summaries.
