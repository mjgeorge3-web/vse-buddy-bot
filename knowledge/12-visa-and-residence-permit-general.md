# Visa and residence permit for study in the Czech Republic – requirements and rules

> General information only. Always confirm current legal requirements for your nationality with the Czech Embassy/Consulate, the Czech Ministry of the Interior (IPC / OAMP), and the VŠE International Office or Admission Office.

## 1. Non-EU / Non-EEA Students: Visa vs. Long-Term Residence Permit
Students from outside the EU/EEA/Switzerland staying in the Czech Republic for more than 90 days must apply **before arrival** at a territorially competent Czech Embassy or Consulate for one of two study authorizations:
- **Long-Term Visa for the Purpose of Studies (*Vízum k pobytu nad 90 dnů za účelem studia*):** Issued for up to 1 year; renewable in the Czech Republic as a long-term residence permit.
- **Long-Term Residence Permit for the Purpose of Studies (*Povolení k dlouhodobému pobytu za účelem studia*):** Issued directly for accredited university degree programmes and exchange studies; requires post-arrival biometric card collection at MOI OAMP in Prague.
- **Student Facilitation Programme (*Režim student*):** Degree and exchange students admitted to VŠE may be nominated by the VŠE Admission Office / International Office for priority embassy appointment scheduling where applicable.

## 2. Core Documents Required for Czech Study Visa / Residence Permit
1. **Valid Travel Passport** (valid at least 3 months beyond intended stay, with at least 2 blank pages) and passport-sized photographs.
2. **Official Confirmation of Study (*Potvrzení o přijetí ke studiu / Potvrzení o studiu*):** Issued by VŠE (in Czech) confirming enrollment in an accredited study programme.
3. **Proof of Accommodation (*Doklad o zajištění ubytování*):**
   - **VŠE Dormitory (SÚZ / ISKaM):** Official electronically signed accommodation confirmation issued via ISKaM (https://iskam-web.vse.cz/) after paying the booking deposit (*kauce*).
   - **Private Flat / Apartment:** Signed lease contract (*Nájemní smlouva*) or official Accommodation Confirmation form with an officially verified signature of the property owner.
4. **Proof of Financial Means:** Bank statement (with an internationally accepted payment card if in a foreign currency) proving required monthly subsistence funds for study stays, or an official scholarship confirmation.
5. **Comprehensive Health Insurance (*Komplexní zdravotní pojištění cizinců*):** Required upon visa collection and throughout your entire stay in Czechia from an insurer licensed in the Czech Republic (e.g., **pVZP**, **Slavia**, **Maxima**, **UNIQA**, or **SV Pojišťovna**) with a statutory coverage limit of at least **EUR 400,000** per insured event without exclusions (pursuant to Czech Act No. 326/1999 Sb., § 180j).
6. **Criminal Record Extract (*Výpis z rejstříku trestů*):** Apostilled or superlegalized and officially translated into Czech.

## 3. Post-Arrival Duties & Permit Extensions in Prague
- **3-Working-Day Foreign Police Registration:**
  - **VŠE Dormitories:** SÚZ VŠE reports your arrival to the Foreign Police **automatically** upon check-in.
  - **Private Accommodation:** Non-EU students must register in person within **3 working days** of arrival at the Prague Foreign Police (*Cizinecká policie*, Olšanská 2176/2, Prague 3 – Žižkov).
- **Biometrics Appointment (Long-Term Residence Permit):** Book an appointment online via the Official Information Portal for Foreigners / MOI Reservation System (https://frs.gov.cz/) to provide biometric data upon arrival and collect your biometric residence card.
- **Extending Your Permit:** Submit your renewal application to OAMP MVČR **between 120 days and 1 day before** your current visa or permit expires via https://frs.gov.cz/, attaching an updated VŠE Electronic Confirmation of Study (`/auth/student/potvrzeni.pl`), renewed Proof of Accommodation, proof of funds, and valid comprehensive health insurance.

## 4. EU / EEA / Swiss Students
- **No Visa Required:** Citizens of the EU, EEA, and Switzerland enter and study visa-free with a valid national ID card or passport.
- **30-Day Reporting Duty:** If staying in the Czech Republic for **more than 30 days**, EU/EEA/Swiss students must report their presence to the Foreign Police within 30 days of arrival—**unless staying in a VŠE dormitory**, which handles reporting automatically.
- **Health Coverage:** Bring a valid **European Health Insurance Card (EHIC)** and register it locally if needed.
