# Academic Psychological Counselling and Mental Well-being at VŠE

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.

## What is Academic Psychological Counselling (APC)?
- Academic Psychological Counselling is a professional, confidential, and completely **free of charge** service provided by the Academic Centre (Akademické centrum) for all enrolled VŠE students and staff.
- **Languages Available:** Full counselling sessions are provided in **Czech** and **English** (Polish and Russian available upon specific request via email).
- **Format:** Consultations are conducted either in-person at the Žižkov campus or online via MS Teams, requiring prior reservation.

## Common Reasons Students Seek Counselling
International students frequently consult the service for support with:
- **Academic adjustment and stress:** Overcoming exam anxiety, deadline pressure, procrastination, concentration difficulties, and loss of study motivation.
- **Culture shock and adaptation:** Coping with relocation to Prague, language barriers, loneliness, and homesickness.
- **Interpersonal relationships:** Communication difficulties with peers, roommates, professors, or partners.
- **Emotional and psychological well-being:** Mood fluctuations, chronic worry, mild depression, acute distress, or life transitions.
- **Personal and career development:** Self-confidence building, decision-making, and coping strategies.

## How to Book an Appointment
1. **Check Schedule:** Review available consultation slots on the Academic Centre booking schedule at https://ac.vse.cz/english/psychological-counselling/opening-times/.
2. **Email Registration:** Write directly to `ac@vse.cz` indicating your student identification, faculty, and preferred language (English or Czech).
3. **Emergency Discretion:** If you are experiencing acute psychological distress, mention the urgency in the email subject line so the counsellors can prioritize your request.

## Professional Ethics and Confidentiality
All counsellors adhere strictly to the Ethical Code of Czech University Counsellors:
- **Full Confidentiality:** Information shared during sessions is never disclosed to faculty administrators, professors, parents, or outside authorities.
- **Non-Judgmental Support:** Discretion, mutual respect, and student-centered support are unconditionally guaranteed.

## Urgent Mental Health Crisis & Emergency Helplines (24/7)
If you or someone you know is experiencing acute severe distress or a life-threatening mental health emergency, do not wait for a scheduled university appointment:
- **General Emergency (Police, Fire, Medical):** Dial **112** (available 24/7 in English and Czech, free of charge).
- **Czech Crisis Helpline (Linka první psychické pomoci):** Dial **116 123** (free, anonymous, available 24/7 for adults in psychological distress).
- **Safety Helpline (Linka bezpečí - for youth & students under 26):** Dial **116 111** (free, anonymous, 24/7).
- **Prague Crisis Intervention Centre (Centrum krizové intervence - Bohnice):**
  - Phone: `+420 284 016 110` (24/7 emergency psychiatric walk-in clinic and phone helpline).
  - Address: Ústavní 91, Prague 8 - Bohnice.
