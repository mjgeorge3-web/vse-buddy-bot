# Academic Psychological Counselling (APC)

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
## What it is
- Free, short-term counselling service of the Academic Centre for all VŠE students and staff.
- Languages: **Czech** and **English** (Polish and Russian on request via ac@vse.cz).
- Sessions in-person or online, with advance registration.

## Common topics
- Study (concentration, procrastination, motivation, stress).
- Relations (peers, teachers, partners, family).
- Culture shock and adaptation.
- Career or personal development.
- Mood, anxiety, overwhelming reactions.

## Code of Ethics
Based on the Czech University Counsellors' Code (professional responsibility, respect, discretion, expertise, refusal).

> Free service. Email ac@vse.cz or check the current schedule at https://ac.vse.cz/english/psychological-counselling/opening-times/.
