# Health insurance and medical care

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
International students must have valid health insurance covering their stay in the Czech Republic. Two main categories:
- **EU/EEA/Swiss students**: use the EHIC (European Health Insurance Card) from home plus supplementary commercial insurance recommended.
- **Non-EU students**: a contract with a Czech-approved insurance company (e.g. PVZP, Slavia, Maxima) is normally a requirement of the long-stay visa/residence permit.

General practitioner (praktický lékař) and hospitals are accessible with valid insurance. Emergency: **155** (ambulance) or **112** (single European emergency number).
