# Health insurance and medical care

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
International students must have valid health insurance covering their stay in the Czech Republic. Two main categories:
- **EU/EEA/Swiss students**: use the **EHIC** (European Health Insurance Card) from your home country (registering it locally with a Czech public health insurance fund such as VZP ČR is recommended for full coverage).
- **Non-EU students**: must arrange **Comprehensive Health Insurance (*Komplexní zdravotní pojištění cizinců*)** with an insurance company licensed to operate in the Czech Republic (e.g. **pVZP**, **Slavia**, **Maxima**, **UNIQA**, or **SV Pojišťovna**) with a minimum coverage limit of **EUR 400,000** per insured event without exclusions (under Czech Act No. 326/1999 Sb., § 180j) as a mandatory condition of the long-stay visa or residence permit.

General practitioner (praktický lékař) and hospitals are accessible with valid insurance. Emergency: **155** (ambulance) or **112** (single European emergency number).
