# Incoming exchange students

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
Information for Erasmus+ and other exchange students coming to VŠE (nomination by home institution, learning agreement, course registration, etc.). The page is the gateway to the International Office / OZS exchange workflow.

Exchange-specific orientation events run alongside the degree-student Orientation Week.
