# Living in Prague – districts, food, transport

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
VŠE's Žižkov campus is in Prague 3. Jižní Město campus is in Prague 4. Both are well connected by metro/tram/bus.

Useful apps highlighted on VŠE International Instagram (Oct 2026):
- Meníčka.cz – daily lunch menus.
- Nesnězeno – discounted food from restaurants.
- LOKNI – free drinking-water refill map.
- Kupi.cz – grocery price comparison.
- Vinted / local thrift shops – second-hand clothing.
- PID Lítačka – public transport.

Always pay in CZK (Czech crowns) to avoid unfavourable Dynamic Currency Conversion on foreign cards.
