# Catering and university canteens (menza)

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
VŠE canteens (menzy) are run by SKM. Daily menus are typically posted at https://skm.vse.cz. ISIC-card holders and Lítačka students get subsidised student meal prices in canteens of any Prague university (legislated discount applies).
