# Catering and university canteens (menza)

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
VŠE student canteens (menzy — Žižkov Italská budova, Jižní Město, and Jarov) are operated by **AV GASTRO, s.r.o.** under **SÚZ VŠE**. Daily menus, opening hours, and meal ordering via WebKredit / MobilKredit2 are listed at https://www.vse.cz/english/students/campus-services/catering-and-menus/. ISIC-card holders receive subsidized student meal prices; campus dining also includes Café Bar Econom, Akademický klub, and microwave zones.
