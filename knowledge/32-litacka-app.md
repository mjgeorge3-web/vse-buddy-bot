# PID Lítačka – ticketing and journey planner app

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
PID Lítačka is the official Prague public-transport app: ticket purchase (incl. SMS-style single tickets), account management, journey planner and live vehicle map.

> Cards issued in 2016 with "valid until 2020" are being replaced from 2026 (per PID notice on 27.2.2026). Carry proof of age/student status for ID checks.
