# Prague public transport – PID (Pražská integrovaná doprava)

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
## What it is
PID is the Prague Integrated Transport system: metro, trams, buses, trolleybuses, ferries, the Petřín funicular and selected trains.

## Useful tools
- Journey planner and live map: https://mapa.pid.cz/
- Mobile app: **PID Lítačka** (https://app.pidlitacka.cz/) – includes ticketing, free 2×15-min bike rides/day via participating bike-share services.
- Ticket pages (English): https://pid.cz/en/tickets-and-fare/

## Tickets
- Short-term (single, 30-min, 90-min, 24h, 3-day).
- Long-term passes (monthly, quarterly).
- Student discounts are available for ages 18–26 on proof of study; check current eligibility on the PID site.

## To/from the airport
See https://pid.cz/en/travel-information/airport-and-stations/.
