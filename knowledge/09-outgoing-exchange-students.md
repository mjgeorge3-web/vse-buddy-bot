# Outgoing exchange (VŠE students going abroad)

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
Step-by-step guidance for VŠE degree students who want to spend a semester/year at a partner university (Erasmus+, bilateral agreements, free movers). The International Office (OZS) coordinates selection procedures (results announcements appear on ozs.vse.cz/english/news-for-students/).
