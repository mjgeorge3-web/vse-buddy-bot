# Wi-Fi and eduroam at VŠE

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
VŠE campus Wi-Fi is delivered via eduroam. Configure eduroam with your home-university credentials (visiting students) or VŠE credentials (degree students) before arrival. The configuration guide (CAT tool / manual settings) is linked from the page above.
