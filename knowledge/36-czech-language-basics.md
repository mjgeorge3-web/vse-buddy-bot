# Czech language basics for international students

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
VŠE programmes are taught in English; daily life is mostly in Czech. Practical tips:
- Learn the basics: *Dobrý den* (hello), *Prosím* (please), *Děkuji* (thanks), *Promiňte* (excuse me), *Kolik to stojí?* (How much is it?).
- Public transport announcements are bilingual on key stations.
- VŠE offers a survival Czech lecture during Orientation Week and credit-bearing Czech for Foreigners elective courses (`sJ`) in InSIS.
- Popular Czech films often recommended to incoming students for cultural immersion include *Anthropoid*, *Waves (Vlny)*, and *Kolya (Kolja)*.
