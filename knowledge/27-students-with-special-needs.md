# Centre for Students with Special Needs

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
VŠE's Centre for Students with Special Needs coordinates reasonable adjustments in teaching, examinations and accessibility. Contact details are on the linked page; requests should be submitted early in the semester.
