# Employment during studies – general information

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
## General information only – verify with the authorities
- **EU/EEA/Swiss students**: free access to the Czech labour market; no work permit required.
- **Non-EU students holding a long-stay visa or residence permit for study**: employment is generally allowed without a separate work permit, but conditions (max hours during studies, mandatory employer notification of the employment office) apply and change frequently.

Critical: confirm the current rules on the Czech Ministry of the Interior website and with the **VŠE International Office** before signing any employment contract.
