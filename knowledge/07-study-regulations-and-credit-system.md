# Study regulations and credit system

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
The official Study Regulations (Study and Examination Rules) are linked from the Study Essentials page; the VŠE credit system is described in a recent Admission Office news post ("Credit System at VŠE – Don't fail your studies!", 20.10.2026). International students should:
1. Read the English Study and Examination Rules (binding).
2. Note the minimum/maximum credits per semester rules (typical: 30 ECTS per semester; 60 per year).
3. Understand re-examination and absence rules.

Final text and figures must be verified on the live Study Regulations page.
