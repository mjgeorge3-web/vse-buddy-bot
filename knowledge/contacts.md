## 3. Psychological counselling (APC)

- Source page: https://apc.vse.cz/english/
- Service is free and anonymous for VŠE students and staff.
- Common topics: peer/teacher/partner/family relations, culture shock,
  career/personal development, mood and anxiety.
- Booking is done via the APC online form (see page); sessions are held in
  English and Czech.
- The exact opening hours, current booking link and individual therapist
  emails on https://apc.vse.cz/english/ should be re-checked before use.

## 6. Emergency numbers (Czech Republic)

Universal EU emergency line and Czech health/medical line – verified as
official numbers used throughout the Czech Republic:

- **112** – single European emergency number (police / fire / ambulance).
- **155** – Czech ambulance / medical rescue (záchranná služba).
- 158 – Police.
- 150 – Fire and rescue.
- 156 – Municipal / city police (Prague).
- Non-urgent medical help in Prague: 155 dispatcher will route you; English-
  speaking assistance is available on request.

(Source for numbers: general Czech emergency-number information; cross-check
on https://www.vse.cz/english/contacts/ or the Czech Ministry of Interior.)
