# VŠE Career Centre, Šance Job Fair and work during studies

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
Career Centre (kc.vse.cz) supports students with career coaching, CV reviews and the annual ŠANCE Job Fair. International students can also search the VŠE Talent competition and alumni-coach networks.

For work permits for non-EU students, see topic 30 (employment).
