# Scholarships, campus building navigation and SafeQ printing at VŠE

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.

## Scholarships available to international students
- **Accommodation scholarship (Ubytovací stipendium):**
  - Financial support provided to full-time Bachelor and Master students whose permanent residence is outside Prague.
  - Applications are submitted online in InSIS under **Personal administration → Financing of study and scholarships → Scholarships (Žádosti o stipendia)** (https://insis.vse.cz/auth/stipendia/?lang=en).
  - Must be applied for twice a year during the designated application windows (typically October and April).
  - Paid retrospectively in quarterly installments into a Czech bank account entered in InSIS under Student Study Financing & Bank Accounts.
  - Accommodation scholarship is a state housing cash benefit, completely separate from booking dormitory rooms on ISKaM.
- **Merit scholarship (Prospěchové stipendium):**
  - Awarded by faculties for outstanding academic performance based on study average in the previous academic year.

## Campus buildings and room code navigation
VŠE classrooms and offices are designated by building prefixes on the main Žižkov campus:
- **RB = Rajská budova (Paradise Building):** Houses the modern atrium entrance from nám. W. Churchilla, lecture halls (RB 101, RB 201), the Admission Office (RB 527), and the International Office (OZS, 5th floor).
- **SB = Stará budova (Old Building):** Houses the Central Library (Knihovna Žižkov, mezzanine), Likešova aula, Centrum informatiky (CI) IT Helpdesk (SB 22/23), and lecture rooms.
- **NB = Nová budova (New Building):** Houses Vencovského aula, faculty study departments (e.g., FIS Study Department on the 4th floor, NB 403), lecture halls (NB A–D), and student dining facilities (Menza).
- **JM = Jižní Město campus:** Satellite campus in Prague 4 (Kunratice) hosting specific courses, study library, IT helpdesk (JM 355), and student dormitories.
- **Room decoding rule:** For standard three-digit room numbers, the first digit represents the floor. For example, `RB 209` is in Rajská budova on the 2nd floor; `NB 403` is in Nová budova on the 4th floor.

## Printing and copying on campus (SafeQ)
- VŠE operates the **SafeQ** centralized printing and copying service.
- Student ID / ISIC cards are linked to a virtual SafeQ printing account.
- Credit can be recharged online via card payment or via cash recharge terminals located inside the Žižkov main library.
- Send documents to the virtual queue from campus computers or web upload, then tap your ISIC card on any multifunction printer across the library and computer rooms to release prints.
