# Orientation Week for incoming international students

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
The Orientation Week is run by the VŠE International Office one week before the start of the academic year to introduce new international students to the campus, study system, the city of Prague and Czech culture.

Typical components:
1. ESN VŠE Prague activities (cultural, social).
2. Study-programme-specific sessions run by programme coordinators.
3. International Office programme (past example: Library lecture, Intercultural Competences, Cultural History of Czech Lands, Culture Shock lecture, Czech Language Guide, Chill-Out Zone, Team Game).

The detailed calendar for the next intake is published on the Admission Office site – check the page above a few weeks before arrival.
