# VŠE Campus Navigation: Classrooms, Washrooms, and Study Coordinators

This comprehensive campus guide provides turn-by-turn walking directions for international students navigating Stará budova (SB), Nová budova (NB), and Rajská budova (RB) at VŠE Prague.


## Stará budova (SB) - Old Building (náměstí Winstona Churchilla 4)

### [LOC_SB_01] Student Card Centre & ISIC Photo Office (SB 01)
- **Building & Floor:** SB | Ground / 0
- **Facility Type:** student_card_center
- **Walking Directions from Entrance:** Enter through the main Stará budova (SB) revolving doors from náměstí Winstona Churchilla. Turn immediately left before the turnstiles. The Card Centre is in room SB 01.
- **Amenities & Accessibility:** Wheelchair accessible, electronic queue ticket machine, photo capture booth.
- **Hours / Availability:** Mon-Thu 09:00-12:00, 13:00-15:30; Fri 09:00-12:00
- **Search Keywords:** isic office, student id card, card center, photo upload, sb 01, card pickup, replace isic

### [LOC_SB_02] Student Menza & Canteen Entrance (SB Canteen)
- **Building & Floor:** SB | Ground / 0
- **Facility Type:** canteen_menza
- **Walking Directions from Entrance:** From the SB entrance vestibule, pass through the turnstiles and take the wide stone stairs down one level into the lower ground corridor. Follow signs for 'Menza'.
- **Amenities & Accessibility:** Tray return belt, salad bar, hot lunch counter, ISIC card payment terminal.
- **Hours / Availability:** Mon-Thu 11:00-14:30; Fri 11:00-14:00
- **Search Keywords:** menza, canteen, lunch, cheap food, student cafeteria, sb menza, eat on campus

### [LOC_SB_03] SB Ground Floor Washrooms (Male & Female) (SB WC-G)
- **Building & Floor:** SB | Ground / 0

- **Amenities & Accessibility:** Separate Male and Female toilets, hand dryers, wheelchair accessible stall.
- **Hours / Availability:** Open during campus operating hours (06:00-22:00)
