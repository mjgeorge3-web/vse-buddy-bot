# Student ID card (ISIC)

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
VŠE issues student ID cards that double as library cards, meal cards and ISIC cards (international student identity). Pickup details and photo requirements are on the ID Cards page under Campus Services.
