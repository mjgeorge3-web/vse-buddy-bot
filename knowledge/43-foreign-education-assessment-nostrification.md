# Foreign education assessment and nostrification at VŠE

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.

## Internal VŠE assessment for admission (edu.vse.cz)
- Because VŠE holds institutional accreditation, it has the legal authority to assess foreign secondary and higher education directly for the admission procedure.
- General state nostrification is **not required** for most applicants if you use VŠE's internal assessment via the portal https://edu.vse.cz/.
- The internal assessment starts automatically after submitting and paying for your admission application in InSIS.
- The internal evaluation is valid exclusively for enrollment into study programmes at VŠE.

## Required documents and legal verification
- **Bachelor applicants:** Officially certified copy of high school graduation certificate / secondary school leaving diploma + certified copy of transcript of grades / hours.
- **Master applicants:** Officially certified copy of Bachelor's degree diploma + diploma supplement or complete academic transcript.
- **Legal verification of documents:**
  - **Apostille:** Required for countries that are signatories to the Hague Apostille Convention (e.g. USA, India, UK, Turkey, Ukraine).
  - **Superlegalization:** Required for countries without the Hague Convention (verified by ministry of foreign affairs and the Czech embassy).
  - **Exemption:** Countries with bilateral legal assistance treaties with the Czech Republic (e.g. Slovakia, Poland, Hungary, Austria, Germany) do not need apostille or superlegalization.
- **Translations:** All documents issued in languages other than Czech, Slovak, or English must have an official certified translation into Czech or English attached.

## General state nostrification
- If a student needs recognition of their foreign degree for general legal purposes in the Czech Republic (outside VŠE admissions, e.g. for employment or public administration), they must submit a formal application for general recognition under the Higher Education Act to a Czech public university with a matching accredited programme.
