# IT support / helpdesk for students

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
The IT support service is listed under Campus Services. Common requests: password resets for InSIS, eduroam configuration, Microsoft 365 access, printing.

For personal support, use the contact details on the live IT support page; the VŠE switchboard is +420 224 095 111.
