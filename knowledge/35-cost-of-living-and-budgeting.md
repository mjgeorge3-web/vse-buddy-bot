# Cost of living and budgeting in Prague – general guidance

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
Approximate monthly student budget (figures change – always cross-check on official sites):
- Dormitory bed: low-mid range shared, often the cheapest option (see SKM).
- Private flat share: ~8,000–14,000 CZK/month outside city centre (varies by district).
- Food: ~6,000–9,000 CZK/month (cheaper with university canteens + ISIC discount + Lítačka discounts).
- Transport: long-term student PID pass significantly cheaper than single tickets.
- Mobile + internet: combined bundles from ~600 CZK/month.

Save money by using ISIC, student Lítačka, daily lunch menus (polední menu / denní menu), and the Nesnězeno and LOKNI apps.
