# Student clubs, ESN VŠE Prague and Buddy System

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
Key student-facing organisations at VŠE:
- **ESN VŠE Prague** – Erasmus Student Network, organises trips, language cafés and the Mafia Nights.
- **Buddy System** – informal mentor pairing for new international students (operated through the International Office).
- **VŠE Ambassadors** – student volunteers who run events, Mafia Nights and the Photo Contest 2026 (announced 17.9.2026).
- **VŠE International Instagram** – https://www.instagram.com/vseinternationalstudents/
- **vse_studyabroad Instagram** – https://www.instagram.com/vse_studyabroad/
