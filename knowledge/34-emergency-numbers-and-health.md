# Emergency numbers and urgent medical help

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
## Czech / EU emergency numbers
- **112** – Single European emergency number (police, ambulance, fire – multilingual operators).
- **155** – Ambulance (medical emergencies, Czech language operators, English via 112).
- **158** – Police.
- **150** – Fire and rescue.
- **156** – City/municipal police.

## VŠE resources
- Internal Control & Audit Department (complaints): jakub.drabek@vse.cz.
- Data Protection Officer: dpo@vse.cz.
- Whistleblower hotline: +420 239 050 505, code 409370460 (per VŠE Contacts page).

For life-threatening situations call **112** first, then notify VŠE.
