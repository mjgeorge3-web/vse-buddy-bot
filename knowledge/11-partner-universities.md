# Partner universities and international networks

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
VŠE maintains bilateral cooperation with universities worldwide (Erasmus+ and non-EU partners). Memberships shown on the English homepage include EQUIS, AACSB, CEMS, PIM, PRME, CAMBAS. The CEMS MIM Master's is hosted in partnership with the CEMS global alliance.
