# Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams, OneDrive) for students

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
All VŠE students receive a Microsoft 365 account with Outlook mail, Teams for online collaboration and OneDrive storage. Access is via https://outlook.vse.cz.
