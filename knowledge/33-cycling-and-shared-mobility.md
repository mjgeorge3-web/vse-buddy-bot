# Cycling and shared mobility in Prague

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
Prague has a growing cycling network plus shared bikes (Rekola, Nextbike, etc.). Through PID Lítačka, students can claim two 15-minute rides per day for free across participating bike-share services. Always lock your bike and register the frame number with the police if stolen.
