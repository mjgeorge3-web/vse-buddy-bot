# PhD studies and research at VŠE

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
VŠE is a research university (Doctoral programmes are listed under each faculty). International PhD candidates should coordinate with their prospective supervisor and the relevant faculty's study department.

The VŠE Library trial "AI-powered Research Assistant in Web of Science" was announced 16.10.2026 – relevant for early-stage PhDs.
