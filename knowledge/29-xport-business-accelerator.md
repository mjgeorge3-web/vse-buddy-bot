# xPORT VŠE Business Accelerator

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
xPORT VŠE is the university's startup accelerator. International students with an idea can join the programme, use the coworking space and connect with mentors and investors.
