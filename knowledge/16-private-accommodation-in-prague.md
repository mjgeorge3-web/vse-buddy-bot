# Private accommodation in Prague – general guidance

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
VŠE maintains an Accommodation Centre page under Campus Services. Common private options in Prague:
- Shared flats (flat-shares) on bezrealitky.cz, sreality.cz, expats.cz.
- Student residences run by private operators.
- Facebook groups "Flat in Prague / Pronájem Praha".

Always sign a written lease contract (needed as proof of accommodation for immigration), verify the landlord's ownership in the Czech Land Registry (*katastr nemovitostí*), and never transfer deposits before viewing the flat.
