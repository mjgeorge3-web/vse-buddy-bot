# International Week intensive courses and elective courses in English at VŠE

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.

## What is International Week (I-Week)?
- **Overview:** International Week (I-Week) is an intensive academic event organized by VŠE faculties (notably FIS, FPH, FIR) where visiting guest professors from top partner universities worldwide teach specialized courses in English.
- **Credits:** Each successfully completed International Week course awards **3 ECTS credits**.
- **Format and Schedule:** Unlike standard 13-week semester courses, I-Week courses are delivered in an intensive condensed block format over 3 to 4 days (e.g. Monday to Wednesday/Thursday with full-day interactive lectures, seminars, and a final group project or exam).
- **Attendance:** Due to the intensive format, 100% active attendance is mandatory across all scheduled sessions.
- **Target Audience:** Open to all degree students (Bachelor and Master across all VŠE faculties) as well as incoming international exchange/Erasmus students.

## How to register for International Week courses
1. **Check Faculty Announcements:** Faculties publish the specific course codes, topics, syllabi, visiting lecturer profiles, and schedules several weeks prior to I-Week on their English websites (e.g., https://fis.vse.cz/english/ or https://fba.vse.cz/).
2. **Registration in InSIS:** Register via InSIS under **Student Portal → Registration/Enrollment of Courses** (or the extra-semestral course enrollment section).
3. **Capacity and Timing:** Capacity is strictly limited (typically 20–30 students per course) to allow interactive discussions. Places are filled quickly on a first-come, first-served basis once registration opens in InSIS.

## Elective courses in English at VŠE
In addition to I-Week, international and local students can register for regular semester-long elective courses in English across several categories:
- **Free University-Wide Electives (cV / oV):** Courses open to all VŠE students regardless of faculty. Students can enroll in subjects from business, data science, economics, culture, or management.
- **Faculty Electives (fV):** Specialized courses offered by your home faculty outside the core mandatory curriculum.
- **Minor Specialization Electives (sV):** Elective subjects tied to your chosen Master's Minor Specialization (*vedlejší specializace*).
- **Foreign Language Courses (sJ):** Language courses for credits (French, German, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Business English, and Czech for foreigners).
- **Sports Courses (TV):** Practical sports classes at the Třebešín and Jarov sports centers (1 ECTS).

## How to find English electives in InSIS
1. Go to InSIS → **Course Catalog (Katalog předmětů)**.
2. In the search filter, select **Language of Instruction: English**.
3. Filter by faculty, semester (winter/spring), and degree level (Bachelor/Master) to explore the syllabi, examination requirements, and schedules.
