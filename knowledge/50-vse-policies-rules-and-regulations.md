# VŠE Core Study Policies, Credit Vouchers & Institutional Regulations

> General summary grounded in the official VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ), Scholarship Regulations, SÚZ VŠE rules, and Czech immigration portals. Always verify binding rules with your faculty Study Department, International Office (OZS), or the responsible official authority.

## Academic Regulations

### POL_001: Minimum 9 ECTS First-Semester Requirement
- **Summary:** Every newly admitted student must obtain at least 9 ECTS credits in their first semester to continue studying.
- **Details:** Pursuant to the VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ), a student must obtain a minimum of 9 ECTS credits (control credits) in their first semester of study. Failing to obtain at least 9 credits results in termination of study.
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ) (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Termination of study by the Dean if fewer than 9 ECTS are earned in semester 1. Confirm details with your faculty Study Department.

### POL_002: Cumulative Progression Requirement (20 ECTS per Active Semester)
- **Summary:** Students must maintain cumulative credit progress of at least 20 ECTS multiplied by the number of active semesters.
- **Details:** Under the VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ), students must maintain cumulative credit progress equal to at least 20 ECTS multiplied by the number of active (non-interrupted) semesters (for example, 40 ECTS after 2 semesters and 60 ECTS after 3 semesters). Falling short triggers credit voucher deductions or study termination if remaining vouchers fall below remaining required credits. Always confirm binding rules in the official SZŘ or with your faculty Study Department.
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ) (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Credit voucher deduction equal to the shortfall; termination of study if remaining vouchers are insufficient to finish the degree.

## Credit Vouchers & ECTS

### POL_003: Master's Credit Voucher Allocation (144 Vouchers)
- **Summary:** Master's students receive 144 credit vouchers to complete a 120 ECTS degree (24 reserve vouchers).
- **Details:** Master's degree students at VŠE are allocated 144 credit vouchers (120% of the 120 ECTS degree requirement) at initial enrollment. The 24 reserve vouchers provide a buffer for repeating failed courses or taking extra electives.
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ) (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** If your remaining credit voucher balance drops below the remaining ECTS required to graduate, study is terminated.

### POL_004: Bachelor's Credit Voucher Allocation (216 Vouchers)
- **Summary:** Bachelor's students receive 216 credit vouchers to complete a 180 ECTS degree (36 reserve vouchers).
- **Details:** Bachelor's degree students at VŠE are allocated 216 credit vouchers (120% of the 180 ECTS degree requirement) to complete their study plan. The 36 reserve vouchers provide flexibility for repeating failed courses or taking additional electives.
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ) (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Exhausting credit vouchers below remaining degree ECTS prevents graduation and results in study termination.

### POL_005: Voucher Consumption for Failed Courses
- **Summary:** Enrolling in a course deducts credit vouchers equal to its ECTS value; failing the course does not refund those vouchers.
- **Details:** One ECTS credit equals one credit voucher. Vouchers are deducted upon course enrollment in InSIS. Passing converts them into earned ECTS credits; receiving a final grade of 4 (Fail) consumes those vouchers permanently, so repeating the course consumes a fresh set of vouchers from your reserve pool.
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ) (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Permanent loss of reserve vouchers, increasing risk of falling below the required graduation voucher threshold.

## Academic Regulations

### POL_006: Mandatory Re-enrollment of Failed Compulsory Courses
- **Summary:** A failed compulsory (oP) course must be re-enrolled in the next semester it is offered; failing it a second time terminates studies.
- **Details:** Under VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, a failed compulsory course (oP — povinný předmět) must be re-registered in the next offered semester. Elective courses (fV faculty/free electives or sV minor electives) do not have to be repeated — an alternative eligible elective may be selected instead. A compulsory course can be enrolled at most twice.
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ) (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Failing a compulsory course on its second enrollment results in termination of study. Always confirm options with your faculty Study Department.

### POL_007: Interruption of Studies (Přerušení studia)
- **Summary:** Students may apply to interrupt studies via InSIS; non-EU students face immediate residence-permit implications.
- **Details:** Students who meet first-semester credit requirements (or face documented serious medical, personal, or parental reasons) may request study interruption (přerušení studia) through InSIS Contact Centre / Document Office. During interruption, student status is suspended. Always verify permitted duration and conditions in the VŠE SZŘ and with your faculty Study Department.
- **Official Reference:** Czech Higher Education Act No. 111/1998 Sb. § 54; VŠE SZŘ (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Non-EU visa/residence permit holders lose active study status during interruption and must consult the VŠE International Office and MOI (ipc.gov.cz) beforehand.

### POL_008: Recognition of Courses & Credits from Prior Studies
- **Summary:** Courses completed in previous studies with grade 1 (Excellent), grade 2 (Very Good), or Credited (započteno) may be considered for recognition.
- **Details:** Students may apply via InSIS for recognition of equivalent university courses previously completed with Czech grade 1 (Excellent), grade 2 (Very Good), or Credited (započteno). Grade 3 (Good) is generally not eligible for recognition. Exact time limits (commonly 3 calendar years for prior VŠE studies) and credit-transfer rules depend on your faculty Dean's Directive — verify with your faculty Study Department.
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ); Faculty Directives (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Courses graded 3 (Good) or exceeding the faculty's eligibility window are not recognized.

### POL_009: Minor Specialization (Vedlejší specializace) Policy
- **Summary:** Selected VŠE Master's curricula include a 30 ECTS Minor Specialization, depending on the specific programme study plan.
- **Details:** Standard Czech Master's curricula and selected programmes at VŠE include a Minor Specialization (vedlejší specializace, course groups sP/sV) typically comprising 30 ECTS and a comprehensive minor exam. Requirements vary for English-taught Master's programmes (such as ISM), so verify whether a minor specialization applies in InSIS Check of Study Plan or with your faculty Study Department.
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ); Faculty Curricula (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Where required by the programme curriculum, failure to complete the minor specialization blocks graduation.

## Examination & Grading

### POL_010: VŠE Grading Scale & 4+ Conditional Retake Rule
- **Summary:** VŠE grades 1 (90–100%), 2 (75–89%), 3 (60–74%), 4+ (50–59%, conditional fail granting one retake), and 4 (<50%, definitive fail).
- **Details:** Under the VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ), courses are graded: 1 = Výborně (Excellent, 90–100%), 2 = Velmi dobře (Very Good, 75–89%), 3 = Dobře (Good, 60–74%), 4+ = Nevyhověl s možností opravy (Fail with right to one retake examination during the active exam period, typically 50–59%), and 4 = Nevyhověl (Definitive Fail, below 50%). Key restrictions on 4+: (1) 4+ cannot be awarded twice in succession for the same course, (2) 4+ cannot be awarded on the last exam date of the period because no retake date remains, and (3) an unused 4+ automatically converts to 4 at the end of the exam period. Confirm course-specific assessment rules in the InSIS syllabus and with your faculty Study Department.
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ) (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** A definitive grade 4 yields 0 ECTS and permanently consumes the spent credit vouchers. A failed compulsory course (oP) must be re-enrolled in the next offered semester; failing a compulsory course twice terminates study.

### POL_011: Exam Deregistration & Unexcused Absence in InSIS
- **Summary:** Cancel exam registrations in InSIS before the published deadline; unexcused absence results in Grade 4 (Fail).
- **Details:** Students registered for an examination date in InSIS may withdraw without penalty prior to the closing deadline set by the examiner in InSIS. Failing to attend a registered exam without timely deregistration or an accepted medical/serious excuse submitted via InSIS Contact Centre / Study Department results in grade 4 (Fail).
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ) (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Automatic Grade 4 (Fail); confirm medical excuse submission windows with your faculty Study Department.

## Academic Integrity

### POL_012: Theses.cz Plagiarism Check, AI Disclosure & Disciplinary Code
- **Summary:** All final theses are checked via Theses.cz; Generative AI usage must comply with VŠE ethics guidelines and be transparently disclosed.
- **Details:** Every final thesis uploaded in InSIS is automatically scanned by the national anti-plagiarism repository Theses.cz. Any use of Generative AI tools in coursework or theses must adhere to current VŠE and faculty guidelines and be transparently disclosed; submitting unacknowledged AI-generated work or plagiarized material is academic misconduct adjudicated by the Faculty Disciplinary Commission under Act No. 111/1998 Sb. § 64–65 (sanctions: reprimand, conditional expulsion, or expulsion).
- **Official Reference:** Act No. 111/1998 Sb. § 64–65; VŠE Ethical & Thesis Guidelines (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Referral to the Faculty Disciplinary Commission, up to permanent expulsion from VŠE.

## Final Thesis & State Exams

### POL_013: Electronic Thesis Submission (PDF/A), Bilingual Abstracts & § 47b Errata
- **Summary:** Theses are submitted electronically in InSIS (PDF/A + English/Czech abstracts and keywords); post-submission corrections use official § 47b Errata.
- **Details:** VŠE operates electronic thesis submission in InSIS (Student's Portal -> Final Thesis, /auth/student/zp.pl). Before locking submission, students must enter both English and Czech abstracts and keywords and upload the thesis in searchable PDF/A format (plus optional ZIP appendices). Once confirmed, the thesis file is immutable under Czech Higher Education Act § 47b; any factual or typographical corrections discovered after locking must be uploaded as a separate Errata PDF via 'Insert errata' (Vložit errata) in consultation with your supervisor. Supervisor and opponent reviews are published in InSIS ahead of the defense.
- **Official Reference:** Act No. 111/1998 Sb. § 47b; VŠE Thesis & InSIS Guidelines (https://vskp.vse.cz/english)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Missing English/Czech metadata blocks InSIS submission; locked theses cannot be overwritten directly.

### POL_014: State Comprehensive Examination (SZZ) Eligibility
- **Summary:** Students must fulfill all required study plan credits (120 ECTS Master's / 180 ECTS Bachelor's) before sitting for the SZZ and defense.
- **Details:** To register for and sit the State Comprehensive Examination (Státní závěrečná zkouška — SZZ) and final thesis defense, students must have completed all required courses and credits in their study plan and confirmed study-plan compliance in InSIS (Check of Study Plan). Confirm exact registration deadlines with your faculty Study Department.
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (SZŘ) (https://www.vse.cz/english/students/study/study-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Missing required credits prevents sitting for the state examination and thesis defense.

## Tuition & Finances

### POL_015: Tuition Payment Account (Česká spořitelna) & Mandatory Variable Symbol
- **Summary:** Pay VŠE tuition to Česká spořitelna (IBAN CZ39 0800 0000 0000 2424 2442, SWIFT GIBACZPX) with your personal Variable Symbol from InSIS.
- **Details:** Official VŠE tuition payments are sent to Česká spořitelna, a.s. (Account 24242442/0800, IBAN CZ39 0800 0000 0000 2424 2442, SWIFT/BIC GIBACZPX) or paid via card/QR in InSIS Study Financing (/auth/financovani/index.pl). You must include your personal Variable Symbol (VS) displayed in InSIS and select OUR for international wire charges so the full tuition amount arrives. Confirm exact tuition deadlines and refund terms in your Dean's Decree, InSIS Study Financing, or with admissions@vse.cz.
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Admissions & Dean's Decrees on Tuition Fees (https://admissions.vse.cz/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Payments without the correct Variable Symbol cannot be automatically matched; unpaid tuition past deadlines can lead to administrative holds or study termination.

### POL_016: Accommodation & Merit Scholarships (Ubytovací & Prospěchové stipendium)
- **Summary:** Eligible full-time students can apply in InSIS for quarterly accommodation scholarships (permanent residence outside Prague) and faculty merit scholarships.
- **Details:** Under the VŠE Scholarship Regulations, full-time students within standard study duration whose permanent residence is outside Prague may apply in InSIS under Financing of study and scholarships -> Scholarships (/auth/stipendia/) for the quarterly Accommodation Scholarship (ubytovací stipendium) — separate from ISKaM dormitory booking. Faculties also award Merit Scholarships (prospěchové stipendium) according to annual faculty weighted GPA and credit thresholds. A valid Czech bank account must be saved in InSIS Study Financing (/auth/financovani/index.pl) to receive payouts.
- **Official Reference:** VŠE Scholarship Regulations (https://www.vse.cz/english/about-vse/information-and-regulations/scholarship-regulations/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Missing a Czech bank account in InSIS or exceeding standard study duration prevents payout.

## Dormitories & Housing (ISKaM)

### POL_017: VŠE Dormitories, ISKaM Portal & SÚZ VŠE Accommodation Rules
- **Summary:** VŠE dormitories are managed by SÚZ VŠE via ISKaM (iskam-web.vse.cz), separate from InSIS; dorm residents are reported to Foreign Police automatically.
- **Details:** VŠE student dormitories (Jarov, Blanice, Vltava, Rooseveltova, etc.) are managed by Facilities Management (SÚZ VŠE, https://accommodation.vse.cz/) through the ISKaM portal (https://iskam-web.vse.cz/) using your InSIS credentials. Monthly rent due dates, booking windows, reservation deposits (kauce), guest rules, check-out protocols, and fee schedules are governed by your SÚZ VŠE Accommodation Contract. Always verify current dates and prices directly on https://accommodation.vse.cz/ or with ubytovani@vse.cz.
- **Official Reference:** SÚZ VŠE Accommodation Rules (accommodation.vse.cz) (https://accommodation.vse.cz/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Late payments or contract breaches incur contractual fees under the SÚZ fee schedule.

## Visa, Health & Wellbeing

### POL_018: Foreign Police Registration, Health Insurance & Free VŠE Psychological Counselling
- **Summary:** Non-EU students report to Foreign Police within 3 working days (EU >30 days within 30 days in private flats); comprehensive insurance is mandatory for non-EU; AC VŠE counselling is free.
- **Details:** Under Czech Act No. 326/1999 Sb. § 93, non-EU nationals must report their place of residence to the Foreign Police (Cizinecká policie) within 3 working days of arrival, and EU nationals staying longer than 30 days in private flats must report within 30 days (VŠE dormitory residents are registered automatically by SÚZ VŠE). Non-EU students must maintain valid comprehensive Czech health insurance from an authorized insurer throughout their stay (EU students carry EHIC). Always verify binding immigration and insurance requirements on https://ipc.gov.cz/en/ and with the VŠE International Office (OZS). For stress, anxiety, or homesickness, Academic Psychological Counselling (AC VŠE, ac@vse.cz) offers free, confidential sessions in English and Czech.
- **Official Reference:** Act No. 326/1999 Sb. § 93; MOI (ipc.gov.cz); AC VŠE (ac.vse.cz) (https://ipc.gov.cz/en/)
- **Consequences / Notes:** Failure to report presence or maintain valid insurance carries immigration penalties. AC VŠE counselling is 100% confidential and never reported to faculties.
