# VŠE University Policies, Academic Regulations and Student Rules

This official repository contains 50 verified policies and regulations governing international students at the Prague University of Economics and Business (VŠE).


## Academic Regulations

### [POL_001] Minimum 9 ECTS First-Semester Requirement
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 12(1)  
**Summary:** Every newly admitted student must obtain at least 9 ECTS credits in their first semester to continue studying.  

Pursuant to the VŠE Study and Examination Regulations (Art. 12, para. 1), a student must obtain a minimum of 9 ECTS credits in their first semester of study. Failing to obtain 9 credits results in an automatic, ex-officio termination of study.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Immediate termination of study. No exceptions or waivers permitted under Czech law.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** minimum credits first semester, 9 ects rule, what happens if i get 8 credits, first semester minimum, pass first semester

### [POL_002] Annual Progression Requirement (20 ECTS per Academic Year)
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 12(2)  
**Summary:** Students must achieve at least 20 ECTS credits in each academic year to enroll in the subsequent year.  

In every subsequent academic year after the first semester, students must achieve a minimum of 20 credits per academic year (autumn + spring semesters combined) to be eligible to enroll in the following year.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Ex-officio termination of study for failure to fulfill study requirements.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** credits per year, 20 ects annual rule, how many credits to pass year, academic progression


## Credit Vouchers & ECTS

### [POL_003] Master's Credit Voucher Allocation (144 Vouchers)
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 10; Dean's Guidelines  
**Summary:** Master's students receive 144 credit vouchers to complete a 120 ECTS degree (24 buffer vouchers).  

Master's degree students are allocated 144 credit vouchers at the beginning of their studies. Successfully completing a 120 ECTS degree leaves a margin of 24 buffer vouchers for failed courses or extra electives.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** If remaining voucher balance drops below the credits needed to finish the degree plan, the student cannot graduate and study is terminated.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** credit vouchers master, 144 vouchers, voucher allocation, how many vouchers do i have, buffer vouchers

### [POL_004] Bachelor's Credit Voucher Allocation (216 Vouchers)
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 10  
**Summary:** Bachelor's students receive 216 credit vouchers to complete a 180 ECTS degree (36 buffer vouchers).  

Undergraduate students are allocated 216 credit vouchers to fulfill their 180 ECTS study plan. The 36 additional vouchers allow for repeating failed courses or taking additional elective courses.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Exhausting vouchers prevents course registration and results in termination of study.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** bachelor credit vouchers, 216 vouchers, undergraduate voucher limit, bachelor ects

### [POL_005] Voucher Consumption for Failed Courses
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 10(3)  
**Summary:** Enrolling in a course burns credit vouchers equal to its ECTS value; failing the course does not refund vouchers.  

Credit vouchers are deducted upon enrollment in a course. If a student receives grade '4 - Nevyhověl' (Fail) or is unexcused, the vouchers are permanently lost. Enrolling in the course again in a later semester requires spending a new batch of vouchers.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Loss of buffer vouchers, accelerating risk of running out of credits before completing the study plan.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** failed course vouchers, do i lose vouchers if i fail, voucher penalty, voucher refund


## Academic Regulations

### [POL_006] Maximum Duration of Study
**Official Reference:** Czech Higher Education Act No. 111/1998 Sb. § 56; VŠE Regulations Art. 13  
**Summary:** Studies must be completed within the standard duration plus a maximum of two additional calendar years.  

For a 2-year Master's degree (standard duration 4 semesters), the maximum permitted duration of study is 4 years (8 semesters). For a 3-year Bachelor's degree (6 semesters), the maximum duration is 5 years (10 semesters).

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Irrevocable termination of study upon expiration of the maximum duration limit.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** maximum study duration, how many years can i study, study time limit, extension limit

### [POL_007] Mandatory Re-enrollment of Failed Compulsory Courses
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 11  
**Summary:** If a student fails a compulsory (obligatory) course, they must re-register it in the immediately following semester.  

A compulsory course (oP) that was not successfully completed must be re-registered in the immediately following semester in which it is offered. Elective courses (vP) do not have to be repeated; an alternative elective may be chosen.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Failing a compulsory course twice results in immediate study termination.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** fail compulsory course, repeat subject, re-enroll failed class, failed op course

### [POL_008] Interruption of Studies (Přerušení studia)
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 14; Act No. 111/1998 Sb. § 54  
**Summary:** Students may request study interruption for medical, personal, or family reasons for up to two semesters.  

A student may apply for interruption of studies via InSIS Contact Centre. The student must have earned at least 9 ECTS in their first semester prior to requesting interruption. During interruption, student status is suspended (no student discounts, visa implications for non-EU students).

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Non-EU students must inform MVČR immediately, as interruption suspends the residence permit purpose.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** interrupt studies, pause degree, take semester off, preruseni studia, leave of absence

### [POL_009] Recognition of Credits from Prior Studies
**Official Reference:** Dean's Directive on Recognition of Examinations and Credits  
**Summary:** Courses completed at accredited universities within the past 5 years with grades 1 or 2 may be recognized.  

Students may apply for credit recognition for equivalent courses completed in previous university studies within the past 5 years. Courses must have been evaluated with Czech grade 1 (Excellent) or 2 (Very Good) or equivalent ECTS grades A or B.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Grade 3 (Good / C / D / E) and courses completed more than 5 years ago are not recognized.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** credit recognition, transfer credits, recognize previous courses, uznani zkousek

### [POL_010] Minor Specialization (Vedlejší specializace) Policy
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 9; Faculty Curricula  
**Summary:** Master's students must choose and complete one approved Minor Specialization (30 ECTS total).  

Master's degree curricula at VŠE require students to register for a Minor Specialization (vedlejší specializace) comprising 30 ECTS of specialized courses and a comprehensive minor state examination (souborná zkouška z VS).

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Failure to complete the minor specialization or its comprehensive exam prevents graduation.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** minor specialization, vedlejsi specializace, 30 ects minor, minor comprehensive exam


## Examination & Grading

### [POL_011] Czech 1–4 Inverted Grading Scale
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 16  
**Summary:** VŠE uses a 4-point grading scale where 1 is the highest passing grade and 4 is failure.  

Grading scale at VŠE: 1 = Výborně (Excellent, 90-100%), 2 = Velmi dobře (Very Good, 75-89%), 3 = Dobře (Good, 60-74%), 4 = Nevyhověl (Fail, 0-59%). Non-graded courses receive 'Zap' (Zápočet / Credit).

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Grade 4 grants 0 ECTS credits and burns an exam attempt.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** grading system, what does grade 1 mean, grading scale, czech grades, passing grade vse

### [POL_012] Three-Attempt Exam Rule (1 Regular + 2 Retakes)
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 17(4)  
**Summary:** Students are entitled to a maximum of three exam attempts per enrolled course in a single semester.  

Every enrolled student has the right to one regular examination attempt (řádný termín) and up to two retake attempts (opravné termíny) within the designated examination period of that semester, provided exam dates are available.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Failing the third attempt results in grade 4 for the entire course.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** exam retakes, how many exam attempts, retake policy, failed exam retake, opravny termin, fail an exam 3 times, failed exam three times, fail exam 3 times, third attempt fail

### [POL_013] Prerequisite Continuous Assessment (Zápočet) Threshold
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 15  
**Summary:** Students must fulfill semester coursework requirements before they are admitted to the final exam.  

If a course syllabus specifies a prerequisite continuous assessment (zápočet) or minimum semester point threshold (typically 50% or 60%), students who fail to meet this threshold are not permitted to register for the final exam.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Disqualification from final exam, resulting in course grade 4 (Fail).  
**Keywords & Triggers:** zapocet, continuous assessment, can i take exam without zapocet, semester requirements

### [POL_014] Unexcused Exam Absence Penalty
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 18(2)  
**Summary:** Missing a registered examination without an approved medical excuse results in an automatic Grade 4 (Fail).  

If a student registers for an exam date in InSIS and fails to appear without deregistering before the deadline or submitting an excused medical note, the examiner records grade '4 - Nevyhověl' (Fail) and one attempt is burned.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Automatic Grade 4 and loss of one exam attempt.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** missed exam, didn't show up for exam, unexcused absence exam, no show exam penalty

### [POL_015] Medical Absence Excuse Submission (5-Day Deadline)
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 18(3)  
**Summary:** Medical excuses must be submitted within 5 working days of the missed exam date via InSIS Contact Centre.  

To excuse an exam absence for illness, a student must obtain a certified doctor's note and submit an official absence request via the InSIS Contact Centre within 5 business days of the examination date.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Late medical submissions after the 5-day deadline are rejected, and the absence remains unexcused.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** doctor note exam, sick during exam, medical excuse deadline, omluvenka, 5 days doctor note

### [POL_016] Exam Deregistration (Cancellation) Deadline
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 17(6)  
**Summary:** Students may cancel their exam registration in InSIS up to the deadline specified by the course examiner (usually 24h prior).  

Students can withdraw from an exam date directly in InSIS without penalty, provided the cancellation occurs before the published closing deadline (typically 24 to 48 hours before the exam start time).

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Attempting to cancel after the deadline is blocked by InSIS; failing to attend results in Grade 4.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** cancel exam, deregister exam, unregister exam date, odhlaseni ze zkousky deadline

### [POL_017] Examination Conflict Resolution Protocol
**Official Reference:** VŠE Examination Guidelines  
**Summary:** If two exams overlap in schedule, the student must notify examiners at least 7 days in advance.  

In the rare event that two mandatory exams clash at the exact same hour, the student is entitled to contact the respective examiners at least 7 calendar days before the date to arrange an alternate time slot.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Failure to notify examiners in advance leaves the student responsible for selecting only one exam.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** exam clash, overlapping exams, two exams same time, exam schedule conflict

### [POL_018] Right to Inspect Written Exam Papers (14-Day Window)
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 19  
**Summary:** Students have the legal right to inspect their graded written exam papers within 14 days of grade publication.  

Every student has the right to inspect their evaluated exam paper, test answers, and scoring criteria within 14 calendar days of the grade being entered into InSIS, and to request a review if a calculation error occurred.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Examiners are legally required to grant access during office hours or by appointment.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** view exam paper, check test mistakes, inspect graded exam, review test answers, nahled do testu


## Plagiarism & AI Ethics

### [POL_019] Theses.cz Automated Anti-Plagiarism Protocol
**Official Reference:** Rector's Directive on Checking Originality of Theses  
**Summary:** All final theses and coursework are scanned against national academic databases via Theses.cz.  

Upon submission in InSIS, every Bachelor's and Master's thesis is automatically indexed and scanned by the national anti-plagiarism system Theses.cz. The software compares the text against millions of academic publications, internet sources, and previously defended dissertations.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Suspected plagiarism triggers a referral to the Faculty Disciplinary Commission.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** plagiarism check, theses.cz, similarity score, plagiarism percentage, similarity threshold

### [POL_020] Permitted vs. Prohibited Generative AI Usage
**Official Reference:** VŠE Ethical Code and Guidelines on the Use of Artificial Intelligence in Studies (2024)  
**Summary:** Generative AI is permitted for brainstorming, grammar improvement, and coding help, but must be declared.  

Students may utilize Generative AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) for conceptual ideation, literature discovery, code debugging, and linguistic refinement, provided the use is transparently acknowledged. Directly copying AI-generated text and presenting it as original student scholarship constitutes academic misconduct.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Unacknowledged AI generation is treated as unauthorized third-party authorship and plagiarism.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** can i use chatgpt, ai policy vse, generative ai thesis, declaration of ai use, chatgpt allowed

### [POL_021] Faculty Disciplinary Commission Penalties
**Official Reference:** Act No. 111/1998 Sb. § 64, 65; VŠE Disciplinary Code  
**Summary:** Disciplinary violations can result in formal reprimand, conditional suspension, or permanent expulsion.  

Under Czech Higher Education Act § 64, intentional academic fraud, cheating during examinations, forged medical certificates, or plagiarism are adjudicated by the Faculty Disciplinary Commission. Sanctions include: 1. Reprimand, 2. Conditional expulsion with a probationary period, 3. Immediate permanent expulsion from VŠE.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Permanent expulsion from the university and permanent notation on academic transcripts.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** disciplinary commission, expelled for cheating, punishment for plagiarism, disciplinarni komise

### [POL_022] Standard Citation Format Requirements (APA 7th / ISO 690)
**Official Reference:** Faculty Thesis Guidelines; VŠE CIKS Library Citation Standards  
**Summary:** All academic submissions in English-taught programs must strictly adhere to APA 7th or ISO 690 citation standards.  

Academic papers, seminar projects, and final dissertations must consistently follow an approved citation standard (APA 7th edition is mandatory in FIS and FPH, ISO 690 in certain technical courses). Every borrowed idea, chart, or quotation must contain an in-text citation and corresponding bibliography entry.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Failure to cite sources correctly results in point deductions or rejection of the work.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** citation style, apa 7th, iso 690, bibliography format, how to cite sources vse

### [POL_023] Fabrication or Falsification of Research Data
**Official Reference:** VŠE Code of Ethics, Art. 4  
**Summary:** Inventing survey responses, experimental data, or company financials is classified as severe academic fraud.  

Fabricating empirical findings, simulating fictitious survey respondents, or falsifying statistical data in master theses or seminar papers is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate disciplinary proceedings.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Disciplinary hearing and invalidation of the final thesis.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** fake survey data, falsifying research, made up results thesis, research misconduct

### [POL_024] Prohibition of Unauthorized Collaboration on Individual Work
**Official Reference:** VŠE Code of Ethics  
**Summary:** Submitting identical code or text on individual homework assignments is prohibited.  

Unless an assignment is explicitly designated by the course syllabus as a team/group project, students must complete their homework and programming tasks independently. Submitting duplicate code or text written by a peer is treated as mutual cheating.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** 0 points awarded to both the sender and recipient, plus possible disciplinary referral.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** copying homework, sharing code with friend, unauthorized collaboration, homework cheating

### [POL_025] Exam Impersonation and Identity Verification
**Official Reference:** VŠE Disciplinary Code; Czech Criminal Code § 209 (Fraud)  
**Summary:** Students must present physical ISIC cards at all exams; impersonation is a criminal offense.  

Prior to taking any written or oral examination, students must present a valid university ID card (ISIC) or government passport. Having another person sit for an exam or taking an exam for someone else constitutes fraudulent impersonation.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Immediate permanent expulsion of both parties and reporting to Czech Police authorities.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** exam id check, forgot isic exam, someone else take exam, impersonation penalty


## Final Thesis & State Exams

### [POL_026] Thesis Assignment Confirmation Deadline
**Official Reference:** Dean's Directives on Final Theses  
**Summary:** Thesis topic and assignment must be confirmed in InSIS at least 1 full semester prior to defense.  

Students must register their thesis topic, formulate aims and methodology with an approved faculty supervisor, and ensure the assignment status shows 'confirmed by supervisor' in InSIS at least one semester before their intended defense date.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Unconfirmed assignments block the submission module in InSIS.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** thesis assignment deadline, register thesis topic, supervisor confirmation deadline, zadani diplomky

### [POL_027] Exclusively Digital Thesis Submission (PDF/A)
**Official Reference:** Rector's Decree on Electronic Submission of Final Theses  
**Summary:** Hard-bound physical copies of final theses are no longer required; submission is 100% electronic in PDF/A.  

VŠE operates a fully paperless thesis submission process. Students upload their final thesis exclusively in InSIS in PDF/A format (ISO 19005 compliant with embedded fonts). No printed hard-bound copies are accepted or required.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Uploading non-PDF/A files will trigger validation warnings or submission errors in InSIS.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** printed thesis required, hard copy diplomka, do i need to bind thesis, pdf/a thesis requirement

### [POL_028] Mandatory Bilingual Abstract & Keywords
**Official Reference:** InSIS Thesis Submission Guidelines  
**Summary:** Both Czech and English abstracts and keywords must be entered into InSIS before submission.  

In accordance with Czech university standards, students must enter abstracts and keywords in both English and Czech into the InSIS metadata editor before uploading the thesis file, regardless of the study program language.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** InSIS blocks the final submission arrow if either language field is empty.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** czech abstract required, thesis keywords, abstract in czech, insis thesis submission blocked

### [POL_029] State Comprehensive Examination (SZZ) Eligibility
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 21  
**Summary:** Students must pass all required course credits (120 ECTS Master's / 180 ECTS Bachelor's) before taking SZZ.  

To be eligible to sit for the State Comprehensive Examination (Státní závěrečná zkouška - SZZ) and thesis defense, the student must have completed all study requirements, passed all courses, and verified their study plan in InSIS.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Missing even a single credit prevents the student from sitting for the state exam.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** state exam eligibility, szz requirements, can i defend thesis with missing credits, state exam prerequisites

### [POL_030] Post-Defense Errata Submission Protocol (§ 47b)
**Official Reference:** Act No. 111/1998 Sb. § 47b; VŠE Errata Guidelines  
**Summary:** Typographical or formal corrections discovered after defense must be uploaded as official § 47b Errata.  

Under Czech Higher Education Act § 47b, the defended thesis file is legally immutable and cannot be deleted. Any corrections discovered post-defense must be compiled into a separate Errata PDF and submitted via InSIS for archival attachment.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Attempting to overwrite a defended thesis is impossible; only Errata submission is legally valid.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** thesis error after defense, errata 47b, correct mistakes after defense, errata submission protocol

### [POL_031] Reviewer & Supervisor Reports Availability (3-Day Rule)
**Official Reference:** VŠE Study and Examination Regulations, Art. 22(3)  
**Summary:** Supervisor and opponent evaluation reports must be published in InSIS at least 3 business days before defense.  

Both the thesis supervisor's evaluation and the external opponent's review report must be uploaded to InSIS at least 3 working days prior to the date of the oral defense, allowing the student time to prepare responses to questions.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** If reports are not available 3 days prior, the student is entitled to request a postponement of the defense.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** thesis opponent report, supervisor review deadline, posudek vedouciho, when can i see opponent report


## Tuition & Finances

### [POL_032] Tuition Payment Deadlines for English Programs
**Official Reference:** Dean's Decrees on Tuition Fees for English Programs  
**Summary:** Autumn semester tuition is due by August 31st; Spring semester tuition is due by January 31st.  

Tuition fees for degree programs taught in English must be paid per semester according to the dates specified in the Dean's Decree: Autumn semester deadline is August 31st; Spring semester deadline is January 31st.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Late payments accrue interest and lead to suspension of course enrollment privileges in InSIS.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** tuition deadline, when to pay school fees, tuition payment date, fee payment due date

### [POL_033] Mandatory Variable Symbol in Bank Transfers
**Official Reference:** VŠE Financial Administration Guidelines  
**Summary:** All university payments must specify the student's 8-digit InSIS ID number as the Variable Symbol.  

The Variable Symbol (VS) is the student's unique 8-digit InSIS identification number. Bank clearing systems at VŠE rely entirely on the VS to automatically credit payments to student accounts.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Payments made without a Variable Symbol cannot be matched, resulting in unpaid status and account locks.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** variable symbol, variabilni symbol, what is my variable symbol, how to pay tuition, bank reference vse

### [POL_034] Late Tuition Penalty & Account Lockout
**Official Reference:** Dean's Decree on Tuition Fees; Act No. 111/1998 Sb. § 56  
**Summary:** Failure to pay tuition within 14 days of notice leads to administrative lockout and study termination.  

If tuition is not received by the deadline, a formal warning notice is issued in InSIS. Continued non-payment after a 14-day grace period results in disabling of InSIS services, course deregistration, and commencement of study termination proceedings.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Expulsion from the degree program and debt collection proceedings.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** unpaid tuition consequence, late fee penalty, what happens if i don't pay tuition, account locked tuition

### [POL_035] Merit-Based Scholarship (Prospěchové stipendium) Criteria
**Official Reference:** VŠE Scholarship Regulations, Art. 4  
**Summary:** Awarded to the top 10% of students with weighted GPA <= 1.50 and minimum 30 ECTS earned.  

Merit scholarships are evaluated annually. Students who obtain at least 30 ECTS in the preceding academic year with a weighted grade point average of 1.50 or better and rank in the top percentage of their cohort receive a non-repayable cash grant.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Failing any single course or taking a study interruption forfeits merit scholarship eligibility.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** merit scholarship, prospecchove stipendium, scholarship requirements, how to get scholarship, good grades bonus

### [POL_036] Accommodation Scholarship (Ubytovací stipendium)
**Official Reference:** VŠE Scholarship Regulations, Art. 7  
**Summary:** Eligible students with permanent residence outside Prague receive a state housing grant quarterly.  

Under Czech state guidelines, students studying in standard duration who do not have permanent residence in the Capital City of Prague are eligible for the quarterly accommodation scholarship (approximately 700-1,000 CZK/month). Application is submitted via InSIS in October.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Exceeding the standard duration of study by more than one year disqualifies the student.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** accommodation scholarship, ubytovaci stipendium, dorm money, housing grant, student living grant

### [POL_037] Tuition Fee Refund Policy
**Official Reference:** Dean's Directives on Tuition Fees for Foreign Language Programs  
**Summary:** Tuition fees are strictly non-refundable after semester commencement, except in proven visa rejection cases.  

Tuition fees cannot be refunded once the semester teaching has started. A refund (minus a 10% administrative processing fee) is granted solely if an applicant proves that their Czech student visa was officially rejected by the Czech Embassy prior to arrival.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Voluntary withdrawal after arrival or class start results in zero refund.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** tuition refund, can i get tuition back, visa rejection refund, refund policy


## Dormitories & Housing (ISKaM)

### [POL_038] Monthly Dormitory Rent Payment Due Date (20th of Each Month)
**Official Reference:** VŠE Central Accommodation Administration (SÚZ) Accommodation Contract Art. 5  
**Summary:** Dormitory accommodation fees must be paid by the 20th day of the current calendar month via ISKaM.  

All dormitory residents must settle their monthly rent by the 20th of each month using the ISKaM portal (via payment card or Czech domestic bank transfer with the assigned student Variable Symbol).

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** A late payment penalty of 100 CZK is charged on the 21st, and repeat non-payment results in contract cancellation.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** dorm rent due date, when to pay dorm, iskam rent deadline, pay koleje, late dorm payment fine

### [POL_039] Overnight Guest Registration Policy
**Official Reference:** SÚZ Accommodation Rules, Art. 8  
**Summary:** All overnight guests must register at reception; maximum 3 consecutive nights; guest fee applies.  

Residents may host overnight guests in their rooms provided the roommate explicitly consents. The guest must be registered at the dormitory reception before 22:00, presenting a valid passport or ID, and paying the established guest fee (approx. 150-250 CZK/night).

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Hosting unregistered overnight guests is a severe breach incurring a 1,000 CZK fine and potential eviction.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** guests in dorm, can a friend stay overnight, dorm guest policy, sleepover rules koleje

### [POL_040] Quiet Hours (Noční klid) from 22:00 to 06:00
**Official Reference:** Czech Civil Code; SÚZ Dormitory Code of Conduct, Art. 6  
**Summary:** Strict quiet hours are enforced in all university dormitories between 22:00 and 06:00 daily.  

To protect students' study environment, quiet hours (noční klid) apply from 22:00 to 06:00. Playing loud music, shouting in corridors, or holding parties during quiet hours is strictly prohibited under Czech municipal law and SÚZ rules.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Fines of 500-1,500 CZK and expulsion from the dormitory upon repeat violations.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** quiet hours dorm, party in dorm, nocni klid, noise complaint dorm, curfew dorm

### [POL_041] Total Smoking and Vaping Prohibition in Dormitories
**Official Reference:** SÚZ Fire Prevention & Safety Regulations, Art. 12  
**Summary:** Smoking and electronic vaping are strictly prohibited inside all dorm rooms, balconies, and indoor spaces.  

Smoking traditional cigarettes, electronic cigarettes, or vaping devices is totally prohibited inside all VŠE dormitories (including student bedrooms, private balconies, kitchens, and bathrooms). Smoke detectors are connected directly to building alarm panels.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Immediate fine of 1,000 CZK plus liability for emergency fire brigade dispatch fees (up to 10,000 CZK).  
**Keywords & Triggers:** smoking in dorm, can i vape in dorm, smoke detector fine, balcony smoking rules koleje

### [POL_042] Room Handover and Departure Check-Out Protocol
**Official Reference:** SÚZ Accommodation Contract Termination Guidelines  
**Summary:** Students must schedule a check-out appointment, thoroughly clean the room, and return keys.  

Upon terminating dorm accommodation, the student must book an inspection appointment with the dorm manager. The room must be cleaned, trash removed, and all university furniture accounted for. Any damages are deducted from the security deposit.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Uncleaned rooms incur a cleaning fee of 1,500 CZK deducted from the refundable deposit.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** check out dorm, leaving dorms, return dorm room, deposit refund dorm, cleaning fee iskam

### [POL_043] Lost Key or Electronic Entry Chip Fee
**Official Reference:** SÚZ Fee Schedule  
**Summary:** Lost room keys or electronic entry fobs incur a mandatory replacement and lock-rekeying fee of 500 CZK.  

If a resident loses their physical dorm key or electronic RFID access fob, they must report it to reception immediately for security reasons. A replacement fee of 500 CZK is charged.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** 500 CZK replacement fee charged directly to the ISKaM balance.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** lost dorm key, lost chip dorm, replacement key koleje, lock rekey fee


## Campus Facilities & Library

### [POL_044] Library Book Loan Periods and Late Fines
**Official Reference:** VŠE CIKS Library Regulations, Art. 7  
**Summary:** Standard book loan is 30 days; late return fine is 5 CZK per book per day.  

Books borrowed from the VŠE Žižkov Library (Knihovna Žižkov) have a standard loan period of 30 days (extendable online in the library catalogue if no one has reserved the title). A late fee of 5 CZK per item per business day applies automatically.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Accrued fines block further borrowing and prevent graduation clearance.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** library fine, book loan period, late library fee, return library book, renew book

### [POL_045] Team Study Cubicle Booking Policy
**Official Reference:** Library Facility Booking Guidelines  
**Summary:** Group study rooms in the library can be booked online via InSIS for up to 2 hours per session.

### [POL_046] Campus Smoke-Free Policy
**Official Reference:** Czech Act No. 65/2017 Sb.; Rector's Order on Campus Safety  
**Summary:** Smoking and electronic vaping are prohibited in all VŠE buildings, atriums, and entrances.  

Under Czech Act No. 65/2017 Sb. on the Protection of Health, smoking and vaping are strictly prohibited in all interior spaces of VŠE (SB, NB, RB), as well as within 5 meters of campus entrance doorways. Smoking is permitted exclusively in designated outdoor courtyards.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Campus security intervention and administrative fines up to 5,000 CZK.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** smoking on campus, can i smoke outside school, smoking area vse, where to smoke

## Visa & Foreign Police

### [POL_048] 3-Day Foreign Police Registration Rule
**Official Reference:** Czech Act No. 326/1999 Sb. § 93; Ministry of the Interior (MVČR)  
**Summary:** Non-EU students must register with the Czech Foreign Police within 3 business days of arrival in Prague.  

Under Czech Act No. 326/1999 Sb., all non-EU nationals staying in the Czech Republic must report their place of residence to the Foreign Police (Cizinecká policie) within 3 working days of arrival. (Note: Students staying in VŠE dormitories are registered automatically by SÚZ accommodation software; students living in private flats must visit in person).

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Administrative fine up to 3,000 CZK by Czech police and potential complications during residence visa renewals.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** foreign police 3 days, register with police, cizinecka policie deadline, arrival police registration


## Health & Wellbeing

### [POL_049] Comprehensive Health Insurance Requirement
**Official Reference:** Czech Act No. 326/1999 Sb. § 180j  
**Summary:** Non-EU students must maintain valid comprehensive health insurance (pVZP or equivalent) throughout studies.  

Non-EU students are legally required to hold valid comprehensive medical insurance for the entire duration of their stay. Under current Czech legislation, comprehensive insurance from Pojišťovna VZP (pVZP) or recognized commercial insurers with coverage exceeding €400,000 must be maintained.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** Lapse of health insurance leads to immediate revocation of the long-term visa or residence permit by MVČR.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** health insurance required, pvzp insurance, medical insurance czech visa, do i need insurance

### [POL_050] Free Academic Psychological Counselling Services (AC VŠE)
**Official Reference:** Academic Consulting Centre (AC VŠE) Charter  
**Summary:** International students are entitled to up to 5 free confidential psychological counseling sessions per semester.  

The Academic Psychological Consulting Centre (AC VŠE) provides free, confidential mental health and psychological counseling in English to all enrolled students. Students facing acute stress, exam anxiety, depression, or adaptation challenges can book up to 5 sessions per academic semester.

**Non-Compliance Consequence / Sanction:** 100% confidential. No academic notation or report is shared with professors or faculty.  
**Keywords & Triggers:** mental health support, free therapist, psychological counselling vse, stress anxiety help, ac vse appointment
