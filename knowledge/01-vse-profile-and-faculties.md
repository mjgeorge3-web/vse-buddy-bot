# VŠE – Prague University of Economics and Business: profile and faculties

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
## At a glance
- Official English name: Prague University of Economics and Business (Vysoká škola ekonomická v Praze, VŠE).
- Public university founded 1953; one of the largest economics universities in the Czech Republic.
- EQUIS, AACSB, CEMS, PIM, PRME and CAMBAS accreditations/memberships (per VŠE homepage).

## Campuses
- **Žižkov (main)**: nám. W. Churchilla 1938/4, 130 67 Praha 3.
- **Jižní Město**: Ekonomická 957, 148 00 Praha 4 – Kunratice.

## Six faculties (EN-website links)
- FFA – Faculty of Finance and Accounting (ffu.vse.cz/english)
- FIR – Faculty of International Relations (fir.vse.cz)
- FBA – Faculty of Business Administration (fba.vse.cz)
- FIS – Faculty of Informatics and Statistics (fis.vse.cz/english)
- FE – Faculty of Economics (nf.vse.cz/english)
- FM – Faculty of Management (fm.vse.cz/english)
