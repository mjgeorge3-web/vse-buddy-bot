# Moodle – VŠE e-learning platform

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
Each course typically has a Moodle space with lecture notes, assignments and announcements. Sign in with your VŠE InSIS credentials; the English language switch is at the top of the page.
