# Sports and recreation at VŠE

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.
VŠE offers sport courses in English (linked from Study Essentials) and access to university sports facilities. Sport courses give ECTS credits and are popular with international students. The PE department details are on the Sports page under Wellbeing & Support.
