# Foreign police registration and residence permit extensions in Prague

> General information only. Confirm with the relevant official authority or the VŠE International Office / Admission Office before acting.

## Foreign Police registration duty upon arrival
- **Non-EU/EEA students:** Must report their arrival to the Foreign Police (*Cizinecká policie*) within **3 business days** of entering the Czech Republic if living in private accommodation. If staying in a VŠE university dormitory or licensed hotel, the accommodation provider reports the stay automatically.
- **EU/EEA/Swiss students:** Must register with the Foreign Police within **30 days** of arrival if their intended stay exceeds 30 days (unless registered by the dormitory).
- **Foreign Police Prague address:**
  - Department of Foreign Police (Oddělení pobytových agend): Olšanská 2176/2, 130 51 Praha 3 – Žižkov.
  - Documents to bring: valid passport, proof of accommodation (lease contract or owner declaration), proof of travel medical insurance.

## Residence permit extension at OAMP MVČR
- The Department for Asylum and Migration Policy of the Ministry of the Interior (OAMP MVČR) handles long-term stays, long-stay residence permits, renewals, and biometric cards.
- **Extension filing deadline:** Applications for extension of a long-term residence permit for study must be submitted **no earlier than 120 days and no later than 1 day before** the expiration of the current permit.
- **Official booking portal:** Appointments for biometrics and document submission must be reserved through the official immigration portal https://frs.gov.cz/.
- **Required extension documents:**
  1. Green application form (*Žádost o povolení k dlouhodobému pobytu*).
  2. Valid travel passport (original).
  3. 1 passport photograph (if biometrics not already taken).
  4. Confirmation of study from VŠE (issued by your study department or printed with electronic stamp from InSIS).
  5. Proof of accommodation (formular *Doklad o zajištění ubytování* or official dormitory confirmation).
  6. Proof of funds (bank statement proving required minimum living subsistence).
  7. Comprehensive health insurance contract and proof of premium payment.
  8. Administrative fee (revenue stamps / kolky).
